Let Claude handle your Outlook.

Connect your personal Outlook email and calendar to Claude. Ask it to sum up your inbox, write replies, or plan your week. You run your own copy, so your email never goes through anyone else's server.

git clone https://github.com/pranav-atreya10/outlook-mcp && cd outlook-mcp && npm install && npm run setup

Needs Node.js 18+ and a free Cloudflare account. Takes about 10 minutes.

What you can ask

“Summarize my unread emails from today.”
“Reply to Jake and say Thursday works.”
“What's on my calendar this week?”
“Add a dentist appointment Friday at 3pm.”
“Make a study session every Tuesday at 7.”
“Find the email with my flight confirmation.”

How setup works

Run the command above

It asks for your email and time zone, then puts your connector online on Cloudflare for free.

Register a free Microsoft app

The setup tells you exactly what to click and paste. About 5 clicks.

Paste your link into Claude

Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Sign in with Microsoft and you're done.

Private by design

You host it

Your copy runs on your own Cloudflare account. No middleman sees your email, including the person who made this.

Locked to you

Only the email you enter during setup can sign in. If none is set, nobody can.

Encrypted sign-in

Your Microsoft tokens are stored encrypted, and Microsoft sign-in uses modern, secure OAuth.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Cloudflare's free plan easily covers one person, and the Microsoft app is free.

Which accounts work?

Personal Microsoft accounts: @outlook.com, @hotmail.com and @live.com. Work and school accounts aren't supported yet. Want to help add them? See GitHub.

Can Claude send emails without asking?

Each tool is marked as read-only or as one that sends or changes things, and Claude asks for your OK before using connector tools unless you choose “always allow.” You can also turn individual tools off in Claude's connector settings.

How do I remove it?

Disconnect it in Claude's settings, and remove the app at account.live.com/consent/Manage. You can delete the Worker from your Cloudflare dashboard too.
